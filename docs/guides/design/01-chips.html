Why the chips look different sizes

In your design every chip is the same height (20 pt) in both rows. The selected row's chips only look smaller: there they are darker than the row (they read as holes), elsewhere they are lighter than the background (they read as raised, and light shapes on dark look bigger). In my first proposal they really were different: text chips came out 18 pt beside 20 pt icon chips, and I gave ⌘2 a border your design doesn't have.
